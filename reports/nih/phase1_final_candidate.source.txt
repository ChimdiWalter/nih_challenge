InvariantCell: time-aware oncology evidence review

Phase 1 concept and feasibility candidate, v14. Internal draft, 27
September 2026. Applicant/team identity, rights and resource
documentation still require review. No submission or registration
has occurred.

An oncology research reviewer needs to distinguish an observation
from a prediction, a proposed correction from an accepted revision,
and a dated source from evidence actually available at a historical
cutoff. A static graph can preserve each statement while obscuring
these differences.

InvariantCell represents assertions with explicit provenance,
polarity, context and separate time axes. A deterministic temporal
engine returns supported answers or specific abstentions. An
independent replay checks a certificate against the exact snapshot
and query. These checks establish reproducibility within declared
assumptions; they do not establish biological truth.

The core empirical result is temporal reasoning on a frozen final
documentary panel: static lookup scored 24/138, timestamp filtering
42/138, and both temporal rules and verifier 138/138. The verifier
contribution is inspectability, provenance, independent replay and
typed failure; no incremental answer-accuracy gain over sound rules
is claimed.

Evidence available includes a four-story, eleven-query documentary
comparison; actual RDF/SHACL/bounded OWL-RL checks; and one
independent image/reference measurement example. A new CIViC corpus
contains 32 real revisions and 238 correlated queries, frozen before
evaluation. Both strong rules and the verifier scored 238/238; all
certificates replayed.

This document addresses temporal modeling (25%), reasoning
correctness (25%), interoperability (20%), feasibility (15%) and
workflow relevance (15%). References are included within ten pages.
It is not submission-ready while expert review and component-level
eligibility remain incomplete. [1]

1. A focused research evidence-review workflow

The intended user is a biomedical researcher reviewing molecular
evidence and pathology measurements for an oncology question. Inputs
are a molecular finding, stated disease/context, query time,
permitted sources and the particular evidence question. Current
demonstrations use public non-PHI material; no patient-specific
treatment recommendation is generated.

Outputs are an attributed answer, supporting or opposing statements,
relevant revision relationships, missing evidence and a replayable
certificate. An abstention names the unmet condition, such as
unknown historical availability, unknown specimen time, incomplete
revision history or missing physical scale.

The workflow separates two questions. Retrospective review asks what
a recorded revision says happened in the past, using today's source
snapshot. Historical-knowledge reconstruction asks what evidence was
publicly available then. A current snapshot containing old event
timestamps can support the first question without establishing the
second.

The new CIViC task asks whether a specific revision had editorial
acceptance at a cutoff and which stored replacement value that
revision accepted. The actual prior and suggested values are
retained. It does not assume that a replacement remained the latest
clinical interpretation or that the parent evidence item remained
accepted.

A completed matched-access documentary M2 selected two of seven
fixed predictions. Native, calibrated, rules and verified policies
each selected one correct answer; rules and verifier selected the
same pair. Verification did not improve this endpoint. The next
scientific question needs real hidden measurements and outcomes, not
a third repetition of this ranking comparison.

Current limits are substantive: no expert-adjudicated clinical
decision set, no validated intervention sequence, no independent
patient outcome and no demonstrated improvement over strong temporal
rules. These are development requirements, not completed
capabilities. [2,3]

2. Temporal representation and unknown information

An assertion includes a stable identity, subject, predicate, value,
context, polarity, state kind and source digest. Observation and
inference remain different state kinds. A source statement is
retained as attributed evidence rather than silently promoted to
biological fact.

Independent axes record event time, explicit validity interval,
public evidence availability, local ingestion and prediction
generation. Ontology extensions also retain publication and
annotation/version times. Missing values remain unknown. Annotation,
ingestion or database update dates cannot fill an unknown biological
event or first-publication date.

Validity uses an explicitly declared half-open interval [start,
end). A missing endpoint means an open bound only when an interval
is explicitly asserted; otherwise validity is unknown. Nonpositive
intervals are rejected. Date-only bounds in older documentary cases
were conservative computational projections, not newly observed
timestamps.

For CIViC, creation and acceptance timestamps describe curation
events. Their intervals describe editorial acceptance of a revision.
Biological time remains unknown. A conservative observed-public-by
bound is recorded after receipt of the current source bodies.
Unknown first-publication times prevent claims of exact historical
public availability.

Corrections preserve predecessor and successor records. A negative
qualifier remains attached to its assertion and is never flattened
into a positive edge. Pending revisions are not automatically
scientific conflicts. Conflicting support and refutation are
reported only within the same stated proposition and scope.

This typed representation is implemented and reusable. It has not
established a learned temporal embedding advantage, complete
causal-path reasoning or arbitrary longitudinal trajectory
reconstruction. Extension requires source-specific semantic
validation and independently justified timing. [2,4]

3. Correctness, matched methods and certificates

A query specifies subject, predicate, context, public-evidence
cutoff, ingestion cutoff and valid-at time, with an optional event
cutoff. The engine excludes mismatched scope, unavailable evidence,
invalid intervals, disallowed predictions and missing or late
dependencies. It returns explicit support, refutation, conflict or
abstention.

A certificate binds the snapshot, dependencies, query, result and
algorithm version by digest. A separate implementation replays the
claimed checks against the supplied snapshot. Tampering or
inconsistent replay fails. A valid certificate can certify a
justified abstention; it does not certify source completeness or
biological correctness.

The fixed comparison uses identical typed inputs: static KG lookup;
availability/ingestion timestamp filtering; strong rules adding
interval and event constraints; and the existing verified temporal
engine. No retrieval, source-access or fitting advantage is assigned
to the verifier. The rule baseline is retained even when results
tie.

New source gold is stored outside the solver interface. All
predictions are materialized and hashed before the evaluator opens
semantic gold. A once-only marker is acquired before comparator
execution; a failed attempt remains consumed and its receipt
persists. Separate development, validation and final-internal-test
files make exposure explicit.

Metrics include exact answer correctness, unsupported availability
uses, invalid-interval uses, curation-order violations,
required-support coverage, provenance, abstentions, certificate
replay and runtime. With unknown first-publication time, actual
future-publication leakage is not identifiable. Missing multi-hop or
biological-conflict tasks produce null metrics, not zero failures.

The v14 saved-proof audit replayed 138/138 final certificates with
source-bound provenance on 138/138. All 552/552 specified copy-only
attacks were rejected; 138/138 repeated replays agreed. All 69/69
abstentions were correct with typed reasons. Observed-cutoff,
interval and curation-event violations were zero. With zero real
contradiction witnesses, detection accuracy is not estimable.
Historical first-publication leakage remains unknown. No answers
were regenerated.

4. Empirical evidence and prospective evaluation

The completed v10 documentary comparison has four source-adjudicated
stories and eleven queries. Static lookup answered 6/11 correctly,
timestamp filtering 7/11, strong temporal rules 11/11 and verified
temporal 11/11. Eleven certificates replayed. No accuracy
improvement over strong rules was observed. These are related
documentary queries, not independent clinical cases.

Older twenty-nine-query results remain archived and unchanged. No
completed benchmark was rerun for this candidate. The v10 labels
were written from primary sources independently of solver outputs by
a coding agent, not validated by clinical experts. First-publication
times were unknown. [3]

The new v13 source is actual CIViC revision history: 32 revisions,
87 attributed assertions and 238 queries. Development has 7
revisions/56 queries; validation 7/44; final internal test 18/138.
Final candidates EID21-40 were specified before acquisition. Initial
molecular families, profile IDs and publication IDs were excluded
from final selection.

Stored prior and suggested field values, curation timestamps and
exact source pointers make each case auditable. One initial therapy
history is discontinuous, so general current-field reconstruction is
not claimed. Current snapshots are not labeled historical archives.
Code and selection were frozen before final intake; final answers
are separate from policy inputs.

V13 exact correctness (development / validation / final): static
7/56, 10/44, 24/138; timestamp filtering 14/56, 17/44, 42/138; rules
and verifier both 56/56, 44/44, 138/138. Both correctly abstained on
119 queries, with zero measured interval, availability or
curation-event violations. All 238 certificates replayed. No
incremental verifier accuracy gain was observed.

Rules took 1.029 seconds; verifier 5.521 seconds. Total run: 9.906
seconds, 71,460 KiB peak RSS. Final labels are now consumed. Queries
remain correlated within 32 revisions; source-field adjudication is
not clinical-expert gold. Historical public availability is unknown.
Documentary correctness does not establish biological utility.

5. Ontology rigor and interoperability

Machine-readable artifacts include an RDF/XML ontology, SHACL Core
shapes and a pinned Biolink mapping. They are bounded original
projections, not complete imports of GO or Biolink. Non-hierarchical
axioms cover source inheritance, inverse revision relations,
symmetric contradiction, value restrictions and disjoint
observed/predicted or positive/negative types. [4,5]

Executed v11 validation parsed the ontology and shapes,
round-tripped 112 asserted data triples and checked graph
isomorphism. Five attributed assertions conformed to actual SHACL
checks. A 169-triple ontology with data yielded an 887-triple
bounded OWL-RL closure, with no implemented-rule contradiction
detected. The run took 4.712 seconds and 49,668 KiB maximum RSS.

Shape conformance and logical consistency are different checks.
Missing required context can fail a shape without entailing a
contradiction. Explicit disjoint types trigger the implemented
logical diagnostic. Full OWL completeness, exhaustive
unsatisfiable-class analysis and all possible datatype/profile
errors remain unevaluated.

GO/GAF handling preserves NOT, other qualifiers, evidence codes,
annotation dates, namespace, obsolete status and replacement
metadata. Biolink identifiers are checked against version 4.3.6.
There is no matching Sample class in that pin; local Specimen
remains unmapped. Broad relationship projections retain exact local
predicates and do not imply complete Biolink instance conformance.

Internal KG formulas are versioned provisional_internal_v1, not
organizer scores. Recorded values are: 112 data triples;
relationship richness 0.611111; attribute richness 1.0; class
instantiation 3/23; property instantiation 14/36; internal
interlinking 5/7. Full unsatisfiable-class count is unknown, not
zero. [4]

The new revision profile passed typed/source integrity checks. It
has not undergone a new profile-specific OWL/SHACL study; earlier
formal results are not automatically transferred to every later
corpus.

6. A real quantity-specific pathology connection

One public NuCLS breast-cancer field provides an independent
image/reference demonstration. The original RGB image and corrected
reference annotations come from the source authors. The fixed
image-only baseline writes predictions before the scorer reads
reference arrays. No parameter was retuned after evaluation. This
asset is distinct from P09 and has no asserted molecular pairing.
[6]

Actual results on eligible references: 46 predicted objects versus
42 reference nuclei; 19 matched objects, 27 false positives and 23
missed objects; object F1 0.431818. Pixel Dice is 0.780564 and IoU
0.640102. Near count agreement therefore hides poor localization.

Eligible predicted area is 18,516 pixels versus 19,928 reference
pixels. At the source-declared rendered scale of 0.2 micrometers per
pixel in both axes, this is 740.64 versus 797.12 square micrometers.
These areas cover eligible pixel-reference regions, not all nuclei,
whole-slide tissue or patient-level area.

Count/localization references and pixel/area references differ.
Rectangle-only and unknown regions are excluded from pixel scoring;
valid rectangles can contribute to object matching. Count is not
inferred from area. Physical units require a declared scale and
source; absent scale stays in pixels.

The typed measurement bridge retains reference count 42 as observed
and baseline count 46 as inferred. Specimen/event and
source-availability times remain unknown, so the temporal replay
conservatively abstains where required. A cross-sectional image is
not manufactured into a longitudinal case.

A later six-field, three-participant trial failed its completion
check. Separate posthoc reference recovery measured two fields from
two participants: object F1 0.2921/0.2951 and count error 39 in
each. Four dimension failures remain; the full patient macro is
undefined. This is not a rescued trial. A new patient-disjoint
benchmark and independent quantity-specific references remain
necessary.

7. Implementation feasibility and Phase 2 package

The Python implementation separates source ingestion, typed
evidence, temporal evaluation, independent replay and optional
ontology checks. Plain JSON records and hashes permit inspection
without a model service. Frozen inputs, outputs and receipts support
offline replay and exact provenance review.

The CIViC acquisition used 1,742,054 payload bytes. New ClinVar
intake added 771,328 bytes: seven exact accession-version responses,
two silently mismatched responses rejected, and two three-version
series. Classification values remain uninspected, gold pending.
Current retrievals of archived record versions are not
byte-identical historical release snapshots; publication times stay
unknown. [7]

The completed comparison used one CPU with a two-GiB, 180-second
bound, without network or fitting. Actual run time was 9.906 seconds
with 71,460 KiB peak RSS. Its once-only attempt is consumed.
Predictions were saved before gold evaluation; the prediction digest
was independently verified. New source review must not be presented
as fresh confirmation of this consumed panel.

Optional formal checks used project-local RDFLib 7.6.0, pySHACL
0.40.1 and OWLRL 7.6.2. The base environment was not modified. Their
licenses, versions and contribution/funding provenance belong in the
final dossier; a successful import is not eligibility clearance.

Phase 2 preparation includes executable code, split data, immutable
snapshots, configurations, dependency pins and documented hardware
bounds. An independent expert review, wider natural-history corpus,
complete profile-specific interoperability checks and a recorded
walkthrough remain pending. No NIH-supplied data is assumed. [1]

Extensibility is tested by preserving semantics across documentary
evidence and pathology quantities, with source-specific adapters.
Further molecular/assay cases must supply actual identity, temporal
scope and independent outcomes; interface reuse alone is not
biological transfer evidence.

8. Assumptions, provenance and readiness gates

Core assumptions are explicit: source artifacts are correctly
identified; current revision API fields faithfully preserve the
recorded curation events; asserted intervals describe editorial
states only; and every negative or inferred statement retains its
scope. These assumptions are auditable but not proven by
certificates.

Known gaps include incomplete historical availability, discontinuous
field histories, no clinical-expert gold, sparse independent
families, no real unresolved biological-conflict benchmark, no
intervention sequence and no independent patient endpoint. Formal
gaps include full OWL consistency and an exhaustive
unsatisfiable-class census. Expected ties and negative findings
remain reportable outcomes.

CIViC content is CC0 and inspected source code MIT. NuCLS data is
CC0 with separately licensed code. Original local code, retained
upstream artifacts and optional libraries require a component-level
rights/provenance inventory. Data reuse permission and non-PHI
status do not by themselves establish challenge eligibility.

The user attests nonfederal resources; documentary compute,
development/support, contributor and institutional evidence remains
pending. Installed dependency licenses and data reuse terms are
inventoried separately, including NCBI submitter-IP caveats and the
local PDF renderer license. Public access does not prove prize
eligibility. Earlier work is not retrospectively cleared. [1]

The Phase 1 window is October 5, 2026 to January 15, 2027. Phase 2
is April 5 to November 12, 2027, and limited to Phase 1 winners.
Registration/terms acceptance and submission require separate user
action; neither has been performed here. No exact closing hour
beyond the published time-zone statement is invented. [1]

Before a submission-ready label: complete source/expert review,
assess the completed rule tie and verification cost, reconcile
current requirements, finish the eligibility and rights dossier,
confirm authorship and verify the final PDF. This candidate records
a feasible bounded prototype and an honest evaluation, not a
clinical or prize claim.
